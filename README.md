# I build across disciplines.

**Independent digital builder · software · product · business systems · design**

I like the part where a real-world problem doesn’t fit neatly into one category. I move between code, product decisions, visual design and operations—then bring those pieces together into something people can use.

My work ranges from business software and workflow automation to websites, brand systems, campaign tools and personal experiments. I’m curious about new ideas, practical about constraints, and happiest when I can take a project from the first messy question to a working experience.

## What I build

- **Software and products** — web apps, internal tools, portals, quoting flows and interfaces for everyday devices.
- **Business systems** — CRMs, deadline and checklist workflows, operational dashboards and automation.
- **AI-assisted workflows** — useful assistants with clear tool boundaries, human review and traceable actions.
- **Digital experiences** — websites, visual systems, content pipelines and campaigns that feel considered from first click to final detail.
- **Early product experiments** — exploring how software can improve hospitality, professional services and other hands-on businesses.

## A few things I’ve been building

- A custom practice-management CRM with client records, recurring obligations, checklists and review controls.
- A connected hospitality demo spanning bookings, owner operations, guest journeys and caretaker work.
- Guardrailed AI operations workflows designed around limited permissions, human approval and audit trails.
- Bilingual business websites and repeatable systems for producing polished, source-checked content.
- A custom Android TV launcher for my own Chroma TV—built as an app that works with the TV’s existing Android system.

## How I work

- Start with the actual workflow and the people using it.
- Bring product, engineering and visual decisions into the same conversation.
- Build an end-to-end version early, then verify the details and edge cases.
- Be clear about what is a prototype, what is live and what still needs proof.
- Treat privacy, reversibility and human approval as product decisions.
